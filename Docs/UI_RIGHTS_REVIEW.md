OpenAI's [Terms of Use, ownership of content](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/) assign OpenAI's output rights to the user to the extent permitted by law. They do not assign third-party trademarks or guarantee that every generated logo is authorized. This distinction is the reason for the narrower residual review below; the entire UI is not held merely because it is AI-generated.
